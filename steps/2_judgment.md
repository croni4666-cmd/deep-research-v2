# Step 2: Set direction and scope

Translate the user's request into a researchable decision or explanation.

- Lock the actor and perspective: who will use the answer, and for what?
- Define geography, time window, comparison set, and important exclusions.
- Disambiguate terms that could materially change the research.
- Decide what evidence would be sufficient for the requested confidence.
- Preserve the user's requested output format and depth.

Resolve minor ambiguity with conservative assumptions and state them. Ask the
user only when different choices would materially change the result, cost, or
authorized scope. Do not expand into implementation, purchasing, outreach, or
other external actions unless requested.
