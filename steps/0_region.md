# Region and population context

Use this step only when geography, jurisdiction, language, population, market,
or culture could change the evidence or conclusion.

Identify:

- the relevant country, region, jurisdiction, and population;
- local languages and likely authoritative local sources;
- whether global evidence can reasonably transfer to the target population;
- regulatory, clinical, economic, or cultural differences that limit transfer.

Prefer local primary sources for local claims. International evidence may add
context, but label extrapolation explicitly. Do not assume a Chinese-language
page is authoritative merely because the question concerns China, and do not
downgrade a source solely because of its language.

Output a short region note for use in later source selection. Do not search yet
unless source discovery is necessary to identify the governing jurisdiction.
