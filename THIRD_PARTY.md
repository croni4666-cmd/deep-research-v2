# Third-party acknowledgments

Evidence Deep Research 2.2.0 has no required third-party Python runtime
dependencies. Its research workflow is informed by established ideas from
research agents, evidence synthesis, and agent evaluation.

Relevant projects and literature include:

- [LangChain Open Deep Research](https://github.com/langchain-ai/open_deep_research)
  for public examples of scoped research workflows.
- [GPT Researcher](https://github.com/assafelovic/gpt-researcher) for public
  research-agent architecture examples.
- [smolagents](https://github.com/huggingface/smolagents) for tool-using agent
  design patterns.
- [DeepVerifier](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15808) for research-agent failure
  analysis and verification motivation.
- [PRISMA](https://www.prisma-statement.org/) for transparent evidence review
  and reporting principles.

These references describe influence and context. This repository does not
claim to reproduce their benchmark results, does not bundle their code, and
does not treat a framework name or benchmark citation as proof of quality.

If copied or adapted third-party code is added later, record the exact file,
commit, license, and nature of the modification here before release.
