# Asterion announces NQ-7 cycle-life result

**Publisher:** Asterion Energy
**Publication date:** 2026-07-14
**Source URL:** `https://news.asterion.example/releases/nq7-cycle-test`

Asterion Energy today announced that its fictional NQ-7 battery retained 96%
of its initial capacity after 2,000 charge cycles. The test was performed by
Northstar Validation Labs and, according to Asterion, independently verifies
the product's cycle life.
