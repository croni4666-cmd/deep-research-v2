# QX-9 registry publication policy

Publisher: fictional QX Certification Authority
Publication date: 2026-01-15
Source URL: https://registry.qx.example/publication-policy

Valid QX-9 certificates are added to the public registry within 30 calendar
days after issuance. During that interval, a valid certificate may exist before
its public record appears. The authority recommends requesting the certificate
number when a recent claim cannot yet be located.
